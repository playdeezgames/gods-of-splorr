Gods of SPLORR!!

Controls

Arrows/WASD: move the cursor (Shift: 5 cells)
Mouse: hover to inspect, drag to pan, wheel to zoom
Click a person: follow them (F or the button to toggle)
Click the minimap: look there
Z / X: zoom in / out · N: follow the next person
Space: pause · + / -: game speed
1-5: cast a miracle at the cursor, or click its button, then the map
P: show or hide the miracles · R: begin again after a loss
M or H or C: this menu · Esc: close it
Gamepad: d-pad moves the cursor · A: cast the armed miracle, follow the person
under the cursor, or begin again · B: the command menu (miracles, follow, zoom,
speed, pause), or disarm a miracle · also bumpers: speed, Start: pause
