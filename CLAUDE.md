# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## State of the repo

Playable simulation prototype. The player is a god with faith and five powers (Manna, Smite, Snake, Smite Bush, Miracle Bush), cast by key or by arming an on-screen button and clicking the map; the game ends when everyone is dead (`R` or click restarts). A 32x32 world with day/night, a calendar and temperature, plus people who wander, forage, eat, sleep, craft, drop things, die and rot. `./build.sh` builds `build/web`; `tools/pages.sh` copies that into `docs/` for GitHub Pages (does not commit or push); `odin test src -define:ODIN_TEST_THREADS=1` runs the native tests.

- `src/game.odin`: world generation (weighted terrain: grass, tree, bush, pond, stone field, dirt), cursor and camera. `src/time.odin`: clock, calendar, latitude-driven temperature and sun, all pure.
- `src/people.odin`: `Person` (sex, melanin skin colour, health, satiety, stomach, energy, sleep need, foraging, crafting and ranged skills, age and lifespan, 2 to 4 hands), wandering, eating, sleeping, death by starvation, poison or old age into corpses that rot into skeletons. `src/food.odin`: berry bushes whose regrowth follows temperature (a god-made miracle bush holds more, regrows faster and bears in the cold). `src/ground.odin`: fiber on grass, sticks on trees, stone in stone fields. `src/items.odin`: items in hands and on the ground (stone, fiber, stick, food; crafted twine, sharp stone, short stick), recipes, dropping and spoilage. Crafted items: there is no knife: a held sharp stone (2 stone, `SHARP_STONE_DURABILITY`, worn by each use) is the cutting tool that skins snake corpses into meat and skin, fillets fish, cuts reeds and sticks and whittles stakes; a spear (sharp stone, stick, twine) hunts snakes. A spear thrust hits with `hit_chance(person.ranged, snake.dodge)`. Spears and sharp stones wear out (`SPEAR_DURABILITY`, `SHARP_STONE_DURABILITY`). Tech tree beyond that: bow (stick + twine; failure loses the twine), stake (sharp stone on a short stick; failure loses the short stick, the sharp stone wears), fire drill (bow + stake; failure loses nothing), firepit (8 stone + 8 sticks, built by `build_fire_pit` on an empty cell beside the builder; terrain `Fire_Pit`, not walkable, `fuel` array holds 8 sticks' worth; a fire drill (durability `FIRE_DRILL_DURABILITY`, worn by each lighting) lights a pit that has fuel (`light_fire`), a lit pit burns `FIRE_BURN_PER_HOUR` of fuel and goes out at zero, and a stick can be added to any pit, lit or cold, up to the 8-stick capacity (`add_stick`; people top up one that is nearly out, and a dead pit gets a stick before it is relit) (`src/fire.odin`).
- Fish and meat: ponds hold fish (`fish` array, slow regrowth); a net (4 twine; a failed knot loses 2) with durability catches them, using a separate fishing skill plus experience (`fish_level`); a sharp stone cuts a fish into 2 filets or a stick into a cooking stick; raw snake meat or a filet is skewered on the stick (`loaded`) and cooked beside a lit pit using a cooking skill plus experience (`cook_level`), and a failure burns the meat to nothing; cooked meals fill `COOKED_STOMACH` and are eaten off the stick (`src/fishing.odin`, `src/cooking.odin`). Catching a fish and cooking a meal pay dopamine at a shrinking rate and are goals (`pursue_fishing`, `pursue_cooking`). A failed recipe wastes only its last ingredient (the twine, or one reed). Ponds grow reeds (`reeds` array, slow regrowth to a maximum) that a person with a sharp stone cuts, wearing the stone; five reeds make a basket, which takes one carry place and has `BASKET_SLOTS` (3) of its own inside (`Person.packs`), holding only food (`stowable`: berries, fish, filets, snake meat); food is stowed there first (`give_or_drop`, `stow_held`), eaten and spent from it, and keeps, while food carried in the open rots (`spoil_carried`) A dropped basket keeps its food (`Ground_Item.pack`; the long-term store): it can be picked up (`take_basket`) or eaten from where it lies (`eat_from_ground`). Two twine make a rope (a failure loses one) and two rope a belt (a failure loses one rope), which is worn at once (`Person.belt`, never held) and adds `BELT_SLOTS` (4) places to `carry_slots`; one belt and one basket per person. Every recipe takes its materials from the hands or from the ground at the person's feet (`have_count`, `spend`, `craft_recipe`). Each successful craft of an item adds a point to that item's `craft_levels`, added to the crafting check next time.
- `src/mood.odin`: dopamine (mood). A successful craft pays `craft_reward` (4x the first time a person makes an item, shrinking with repeats of it and with every success at anything); it bleeds off with a 12 h half-life. Below `CRAVING_BELOW` a fed person has a goal (`pursue_goal`): the item that would feel best, working back through recipes (`next_step`) and walking to raw materials, instead of doing whatever is nearest. Building a firepit takes 16 items, so `pursue_pit` picks a site, hauls materials there trip by trip into a pile at the person's feet (`site_x`, `site_y`) and builds once it is big enough. Hunger comes first: a hungry person (`hungry`: satiety under `SEEK_MIN_SATIETY`, stomach nearly empty) goes to the nearest berries or fresh ground food (`pursue_food`), and with none, or in the cold (below `PREPARE_BELOW_C`), works at fish, fire and cooking. People only eat below `EAT_BELOW_SATIETY`, carry at most one spare berry unless hungry, and do not idly drop nets, cooking sticks, fire drills or catch. Each turn is `take_turn` in `src/people.odin`, which records `Person.doing` (an `Activity`) for the status panel. Bushes have `health` (`BUSH_MAX_HEALTH`): a fumbled forage costs `BUSH_FUMBLE_DAMAGE`, health mends slowly, and at zero the bush is destroyed and its whole berries fall (`damage_bush`).
- `src/thermal.odin`: cold and heat. Below `COLD_BELOW_C` (10) a person's `chill` builds, above `HOT_ABOVE_C` (30) their `heat` does; at `MAX_THERMAL` the full load starts `hypothermia` / `heat_stress`, which cost health in proportion to their level. Chill and heat bleed off fast in comfortable weather, hypothermia and heat stress slowly (`STRESS_RECOVER_PER_HOUR`), and while either lingers `affliction` makes energy drain faster and refill slower (more rest needed). A lit fire pit adds `FIRE_WARMTH_C` to the air within `WARM_RANGE` (`fire_warmth_c`), fading with distance, so it keeps the cold off but worsens heat. `step_thermal` runs from `update_vitals`. A game opens in late spring (`START_YEAR_FRACTION`, set by `set_start_time` on init, restart and `configure`), not at day 0, which is a cold winter day.
- Fire priority and deaths: `pursue_fire` (`src/mood.odin`, tried right after hunger) has the first person alive (`fire_builder`) build a pit, get a fire drill, and light it when the air is under `FIRE_LIGHT_BELOW_C`. Deaths are classed by `Death_Cause` (starvation, poison, cold, heat, old age, smitten): each source adds to `Person.harm`, `death_cause` blames the biggest, and `Game.deaths` and `Corpse.cause` record it.
- `src/roles.odin`: division of labour. Each person takes a `Role` (forager, toolmaker, firekeeper, fisher, hunter, then more foragers; `assign_roles` fills `ROLE_PATTERN` by best aptitude, on init and after each death; one person alone stays a generalist). Things are kept on the ground at the stockpile cell (`Game.stockpile_x/y`, the first person's start), which crafting and cooking use directly: foragers haul fiber, stone and sticks to it (`pursue_forager`), the toolmaker makes a sharp stone, net, fire drill and cooking stick there and leaves them (`pursue_toolmaker`), the fisher fetches the net and brings catches back (`pursue_fisher`), the firekeeper builds the pit beside it, lights it, fillets, cooks and leaves cooked sticks out for anyone hungry (`pursue_firekeeper`; `Ground_Item.loaded`). Specialists skip the generalist fire/goal/opportunistic-crafting steps; hunger still comes first for everyone, and `avoid_fire_heat` moves overheating people off a fire.
- Cold survival (`src/thermal.odin`, `src/roles.odin`): in cold air a chilled or tired person walks to a lit fire and stays in its warmth (`seek_warmth`: chill over `SEEK_WARMTH_CHILL` or energy under `SLEEPY_ENERGY`); the firekeeper lights the pit only when it is cold (`FIRE_LIGHT_BELOW_C`), a stick burns four hours (`FIRE_BURN_PER_HOUR` 0.25), trees hold `STICKS_PER_TREE` 5 and regrow `STICK_REGROW_PER_HOUR` 0.01, and things lying within one cell of a person are in reach (`in_reach`). Chill builds at `COLD_PER_DEGREE_HOUR` (0.25) less what the body fights off (`BODY_WARMTH_PER_HOUR`, plus `SLEEP_WARMTH_PER_HOUR` asleep), and a fire or comfortable air clears it at 4 an hour. At latitude 40 the daily mean falls to about -5 C by day ~290 with nights near -13 C; fur coats and the dried-food store carry tribes through it. 16-world sim (3 years, no powers): 9 of 16 tribes alive at the end, mean 1.94 years, no deaths from cold; the rest all starve in their first year (a berry-poor first summer, or a first store too small for the winter), and every tribe that lives through its first winter lasts all three years. The sim harness must call `update_fire` (earlier ones did not, so fires never burned down).
- Walking: `step_toward` (`src/snakes.odin`) takes the shortest route round water, trees and pits (breadth-first search over static buffers sized for 128x128), falling back to a greedy step only if there is no route; the old greedy version left people stuck against ponds for days. A firepit goes on any free neighbouring cell, and whatever lay on it (a drying rack, say) is moved to the builder's feet, not lost (`pit_spot`, `build_fire_pit`).
- `src/rabbits.odin`: rabbits (`Rabbit`, `Game.rabbits`), `RABBITS_PER_1024_CELLS` at world start on grass. They graze grass fiber (`graze`, `RABBIT_BITE` per nibble; `take_fiber` can strip a cell to dirt), hop toward fiber when hungry (`hop_to_grass`), starve to a rabbit corpse after `RABBIT_FULL_HOURS` without it, bolt from people within `RABBIT_FEAR` (`hop_away`, `RABBIT_BOLT_CHANCE`; the rest of the time they freeze), breed when fed and the season's mean is over `RABBIT_BREED_ABOVE_C` up to `RABBIT_MAX_PER_1024_CELLS`, and with fewer than `RABBIT_FEW` left one wanders in every `RABBIT_ARRIVE_HOURS`. They block cells like snakes (`can_enter`). A spear thrust (`hunt_rabbit`, `hit_chance` against the rabbit's dodge) leaves a rabbit corpse; a sharp stone skins it (`skin_rabbit`) into rabbit meat (stowable, cooked on a stick like other meat) and, if the crafting roll succeeds, fur. `COAT_FUR` (3) fur and a twine make a fur coat (`craft_coat`; a failure loses the twine), worn at once by one who has none (`Person.coat`, `put_on_coat` also takes one in reach or, as the year cools, walks to one), which adds `COAT_WARMTH_C` (15) to the air for the cold only (`step_thermal`); a dead person's coat is left on the ground. The `Hunter` role (aptitude `ranged`) takes the spear from the stockpile, hunts within `HUNT_RABBIT_RANGE` only while there are more rabbits than the starting count (a breeding stock is left), carries kills home and otherwise hauls like a forager (`pursue_hunter`); the toolmaker makes the one spear (`tribe_has`), skins corpses at the stockpile and sews coats while `coats_wanted` > 0.
- `src/preserve.odin`: the winter store. `RACK_STICKS` (4) sticks make a drying rack (`craft_drying_rack`; a failure breaks one stick), a ground item at the stockpile; the toolmaker builds one when `want_rack` (none, or all full while `store_short`, up to `MAX_RACKS`). Berries and raw meat hung on a rack (`set_out_to_dry`, `dry_meat_at_stockpile`; `RACK_CAPACITY` 6 pieces each) lie on its cell as `.Drying` (what it is in `loaded`) and become dried berries or dried meat after `DRY_HOURS` (`update_drying`, from `update_ground_items`); dried food never spoils and fills `FOOD_STOMACH` or `DRIED_MEAT_STOMACH`. While the store is short (`store_short`: under `STORE_PER_PERSON` per person, berries growing, and at least `STORE_SPARE_BERRIES_PER_PERSON` per person on the bushes), foragers (and a hunter with no rabbit about) pick berries for it ahead of topping up raw materials that are over `HAUL_URGENT` of target (`gather_for_store`), and any specialist with nothing to do in their role does the same (`take_turn`, `Storing_Food`); with no lit fire the firekeeper hangs the stockpile's raw meat on a rack and leaves the cooking stick at the stockpile, and anyone laying in berries with hands full of idle tools (fire drill, empty cooking stick) puts one down there first. Nobody eats from the store (`store_open`, `store_season`, `pursue_food`, `eat_from_ground`) unless the season's mean is under `BERRY_MIN_TEMP_C` or they are under `STORE_EMERGENCY_SATIETY`, and no bush within `STORE_BUSH_RANGE` has berries.
- `src/snakes.odin`: poisonous snakes that hunt people and poison them (health drains over time); people flee unless armed with a spear. `src/powers.odin`: faith and the powers.
- Each of these has a native `*_test.odin` (`#+build !js`). `src/web.odin` (`#+build js`) is the drawing and the exported `step`/`on_*` procs.
- UI layout: a 16:9 stage with a square map canvas (`MAP_PX`, 720) in the middle and a panel down each side. Left: clock/faith and the collapsible miracle buttons (`P`). Right: the minimap canvas (click or drag to centre the view there: `on_minimap`), zoom, follow and menu buttons, and the status panel. The map shows `game.zoom` cells across (default `DEFAULT_ZOOM` 15, `MIN_ZOOM` 5, up to the world's size; wheel or `Z`/`X`, `on_zoom`); `fit_view`, `center_camera`, `zoom_view` in `src/game.odin`. Clicking a person, or `F`, follows them (`Game.following` is a `Person.id`; `update_follow` centres the view each frame; moving the cursor or panning drops it; `N` follows the next person) and the status panel then describes them, including what they are doing. The controls blurb and the Urizen credits live in the menu (`M`/`H`/`C`); only the itch.io and more-games links stay under the stage. The game calls `use_canvas(0|1)` to draw on the map or the minimap, and `set_view(zoom, following)` to tell the page the zoom.
- `web/index.html`: canvas shim, tint drawing (one reused scratch canvas, never a canvas per colour: see the vault's `Gotchas.md`), the info/clock overlays, the credits overlay (`C`), and all input (keys, mouse, gamepad), which calls exported `on_*` procs.
- `runWasm` is async: call `memory.exports` only inside `.then`. Calendar parameters come from the URL: `?day=<real seconds per game day>&dpm=&mpy=&lat=` (default 1440 s/day, 30 days, 12 months, latitude 40; months per year is rounded up to a multiple of 4). `?world=screen` gives a screen-sized 20x15 world for testing (`?world=N` an N-by-N one); worlds are `width` by `height`, not necessarily square.

## What this is

"Gods of SPLORR!!": a **god game** on a square grid world. Supported world sizes will be 32x32, 64x64 and 128x128, but **work strictly at 32x32 first**; keep the size a parameter rather than a hard-coded constant so the larger sizes are a later switch, not a rewrite.

## Planned stack

- **Language/target:** Odin compiled to `js_wasm32` (Odin's own `core/sys/wasm/js/odin.js` runtime, no emscripten), drawn on a 2D canvas via a small JS shim. The user's `odin` is at `~/.local/bin/odin`.
- **Graphics:** the **Urizen 1-bit tileset** (vurmux, CC0, v2.0), recolouring allowed. Tiles are 12 px with a 1 px gap (tile (col,row) starts at pixel `13*col+1, 13*row+1`); each tile is one colour on opaque black, so black must be knocked out as transparent at load time. ## Build/run recipe (wired up as `build.sh`)

```bash
odin build src -target:js_wasm32 -out:build/web/game.wasm -o:speed
cp "$(odin root)/core/sys/wasm/js/odin.js" build/web/
python3 -m http.server -d build/web 8000   # wasm must be served over http, not file://
```

Native tests: `odin test src -define:ODIN_TEST_THREADS=1`; one test with `-define:ODIN_TEST_NAMES=<package>.<proc>`.

## Architecture intent

Follow the vault's "pure logic, native tests" split so the simulation can be tested without a browser:

- Game rules and world state: no browser imports, runs and tests natively. Time advances via a `tick(dt)` (seconds) called from the frame loop.
- Browser glue (`foreign import`, input listeners, drawing, exported `step`): one file behind `#+build js`. Test files get `#+build !js` (`core:testing` pulls in `core:os`, which panics on wasm).
- Odin gotchas that bite here: seed the RNG yourself in `main`; constant arrays cannot be indexed by a runtime value (declare with `:=`); event callbacks need `context = ctx` set.
